\section{Why OpenAlex alone is not enough}

OpenAlex is huge ($\sim$250M works) but it does not have everything, and sometimes it is
missing abstracts. The idea: let the user \textbf{connect their own accounts} (``Connect with
Google'' style) and see papers the free database does not bring.

\section{The connectors (froth/sources.py)}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\textbf{Source} & \textbf{Requires} & \textbf{What it adds} \\
\hline
OpenAlex & nothing (key optional) & the BASE: abstracts $+$ references \\
Crossref & just an email & massive publisher metadata \\
Semantic Scholar & FREE key (optional) & abstracts $+$ open access PDFs \\
Scopus (Elsevier) & institutional key & Elsevier's curated catalogue \\
ResearchGate & \multicolumn{2}{l}{\textbf{NO}: no public API; its ToS forbids scraping} \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Design principles:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{OpenAlex rules}: it is the preferred record (the richest); the extras only ADD
        papers we did not have.
  \item \textbf{Dedup by DOI and then by normalised title}: the same paper arrives through
        several sources and must count once.
  \item \textbf{Soft failure}: if a source is down or rate-limits you, it says so and the pull
        carries on with the others. A connector never takes the harvest down.
  \item \textbf{The keys are yours and local}: they live in \codigo{.froth\_keys}
        (gitignored). On the public website they will live only in your browser session.
        Froth never holds other people's keys on a server.
\end{itemize}

\section{The first multi-source harvest (real numbers)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Crossref: 227 raw results $\rightarrow$ the dedup recognised \textbf{58 duplicates} of
        OpenAlex and contributed \textbf{169 new ones} (after the abstract filter: corpus 126
        $\rightarrow$ \textbf{157}).
  \item Semantic Scholar without a key: immediate rate limit $\rightarrow$ it \textbf{failed
        softly} exactly as designed (with the free key it is stable).
  \item The enlarged corpus revealed an \textbf{emerging subtopic}: bubble and fine-particle
        physics ($+$ bastnaesite), buried before, now a cluster of its own. More data
        $\rightarrow$ more resolution on the map.
  \item The relevance filter discarded only 6: the extra harvest came in clean. The
        self-washing of Note 17 scaled without being touched.
\end{itemize}

\begin{resumen}
sources.py adds Crossref/S2/Scopus to OpenAlex with DOI-then-title dedup and soft failure; the
keys are local and the user's (the ``Connect institutional account'' panel). First harvest:
$+31$ net papers and a new subtopic emerged. ResearchGate is out by design (no legal API).
\end{resumen}
