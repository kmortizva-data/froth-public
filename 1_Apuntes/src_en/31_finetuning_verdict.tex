\cabecera{31}{The fine-tuning verdict: specialisation, not magic}

\section{The experiment (6.6)}

Two models competed over the cohort's 22 corpora with the SAME exam (same data, same UMAP seed,
granularity chosen by DBCV): generic SPECTER against \textbf{specter-mineral}, your model
fine-tuned in 83 seconds of GPU with $\sim$1250 title$\leftrightarrow$abstract pairs.

\section{The numbers (the story is in the variance)}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{specter-mineral won 14 of 22 topics} on DBCV validity.
  \item But the \emph{mean} delta was $+0.007$ with $\sigma = 0.170$, a flat average hiding a
        strong pattern:
  \item \textbf{It won big in the core of the domain}: hydrometallurgical recycling ($+0.30$),
        XCT slags ($+0.24$), Beauvoir core imaging ($+0.20$), your thesis ($+0.05$).
  \item \textbf{It lost at the periphery}: geopolymer mortars ($-0.43$), refinery simulation
        ($-0.27$), AI on conveyor belts ($-0.21$).
\end{itemize}

\begin{concepto}{the lesson: a small fine-tune specialises towards the centre of mass}
With little data, fine-tuning does not ``improve the model in general'': it \textbf{drags it
towards where the mass of the training corpus is} (flotation/geology). Topics near that mass
gain resolution; distant ones lose it. Open hypothesis (your natural next experiment): does the
2$\times$ corpus with Scopus reduce the peripheral penalty?
\end{concepto}

\section{The double blind (your moment)}

Two cluster listings from the Beauvoir corpus, labelled A/B at random, key hidden. You chose
\textbf{B} ``because it separated fines flotation from lepidolite collector chemistry'', and B
was \textbf{your model}. Metric and expert agreed on the domain you master: the strongest
validation available.

\begin{ojo}
A ``no improvement on average'' with clear structure (wins here, loses there, and we know why)
is a BETTER scientific result than an unexplained ``$+2\%$ everywhere''. Do not inflate:
qualify.
\end{ojo}

\begin{resumen}
14 wins out of 22, mean delta $\approx 0$, large $\sigma$: a small fine-tune specialises
towards the core of the corpus at the cost of the periphery, confirmed by the metric (DBCV) and
by a double-blind test where you picked your own model without knowing it.
\end{resumen}
